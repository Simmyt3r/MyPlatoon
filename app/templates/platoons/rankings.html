How the strength score works

30% profile completion (60%+ readiness), 25% recent MyPlatoon activity (past 90 days), 20% verified public contributions (one contribution credit per member), and 25% member mini ratings (1–5 stars, Bayesian-smoothed). All participation factors are divided by the number of approved, active members, so sheer platoon size cannot dominate.

A platoon receives an official rank on this platform only after at least {{ min_members }} approved members and {{ min_ratings }} eligible ratings. Others are provisional. One person can rate only their real platoon, and a rating may be updated. Scores are independent of NYSC and cannot prove physical strength, quality of service or camp competition results.
