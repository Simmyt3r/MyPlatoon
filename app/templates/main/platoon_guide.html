What is a platoon in NYSC?

A platoon is the smaller group of corps members you belong to during the NYSC orientation camp. It helps the camp organise people for activities, teamwork and friendly competitions.

Think of the camp as a whole school and your platoon as your class. Other platoons are in the same camp, but your platoon is the team you will often train and take part in activities with.

Before camp (PCM)

You do not need to guess a platoon. Join MyPlatoon as a PCM and your profile will display Awaiting camp assignment.

At orientation camp

Follow camp officials' registration instructions to find out your actual platoon number. You may participate in drills, sanitation, sports and other inter-platoon activities.

After orientation

Your camp platoon becomes part of your service history. Keep in touch with those people during your PPA, CDS and after passing out.

Why MyPlatoon asks for more than a number

Different camps and batches can all have a “Platoon 5”. To place you in the right MyPlatoon community, we ask for:

  • Orientation camp state
  • Service year
  • Batch (A, B or C) and stream (I or II)
  • Platoon number assigned at camp

These details are self-reported on MyPlatoon, not official NYSC verification. You can correct them in your profile. Access to private platoon content is limited to members assigned to the same recorded cohort, subject to profile approval.

What does PCM mean?

PCM means Prospective Corps Member: a graduate preparing for, or going through, NYSC mobilisation before becoming a serving corps member. You are welcome here before your platoon is known.

NYSC's orientation course ordinarily lasts three weeks. For current rules, dates and camp requirements, always check the official NYSC website.
