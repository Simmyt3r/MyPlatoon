About MyPlatoon

{{ APP_NAME }} is an independent nationwide NYSC community platform for prospective corps members (PCMs), serving corps members and ex-corps members. Connect using your actual orientation platoon, or join as a PCM while awaiting assignment.

Members can create profiles, showcase skills and businesses, and decide whether their profile and contact details are public, private or limited to their own platoon. Platoon-specific albums bring campmates together across time. A PCM can join before NYSC assigns any platoon number.

What is an NYSC platoon? · NERD mobilisation guide

MyPlatoon is not an official NYSC or NERD service. All camp details are self-reported until independently reviewed.
