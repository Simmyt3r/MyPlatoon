Then follow these steps

  1. Confirm your institution's process. Ask your school for its NERD focal person, institution-issued email and any required unified access credentials.
  2. Visit the official portal. Follow the registration or enrolment guidance for students and graduates. Institutional validation may be required.
  3. Prepare the academic output your school requires. NERD covers theses, projects, dissertations, SIWES reports and other academic material. Check the exact submission requirement with your institution.
  4. Complete submission through NERD. Where applicable, retain the official item URI and follow NERD instructions for the receipt or submission certification.
  5. Check NYSC mobilisation requirements. Verify your status through official NYSC channels; MyPlatoon does not determine mobilisation eligibility.
